Hardware builds it. The platform runs it.

ZMD Edge Devices and ApexFabric are designed together, but they do different jobs. Knowing the difference is the whole story.

// HARDWARE

ZMD Edge Devices

The physical infrastructure — cameras, sensors, and servers — deployed on-site to capture and process data at the source.

  • Dual/single-lens cameras & parking sensors
  • 2U/2S Xeon and edge servers
  • Custom edge devices, built in-house
  • Always visually identifiable as ZMD hardware
// PLATFORM

ApexFabric

The Physical AI platform powering ZMD AI Solutions, optimized for ZMD Edge devices — models, orchestration, and dashboards.

  • Pre-trained AI models (human, fire, traffic detection)
  • Real-time inference & orchestration
  • Custom dashboards & analytics
  • Purpose-built for ZMD Edge Devices
